# Mozilla Fellowship — eligibility and host validation gates

**Status: unresolved; do not self-certify legal eligibility yet.**

## 1. Applicant location and payment eligibility

The 2027–2028 RFP requires each applicant to be legally able to receive grant funds from Mozilla Foundation, a U.S.-based 501(c)(3). The current Letter of Intent asks for the applicant's actual primary location during the Fellowship and a Yes/No legal-eligibility acknowledgement.

Mozilla's 2027 FAQ adds that:
- funds are paid to individuals, not organisations;
- Fellows generally receive grant payments (some regions may use an independent-contractor arrangement);
- the Fellow needs a bank account in their own name that can accept U.S. funds;
- by applying, the Fellow confirms they are eligible to receive grant and stipend funds in the country where they reside and will work;
- Mozilla cannot advise on an individual's visa/work-permit or local-law position.

The applicant's stated residence is Syria. The public FAQ does not provide a Syria-specific determination. Do not infer that Syria is eligible or ineligible from nationality alone, and do not answer the LOI legal-eligibility question Yes until Mozilla provides a clear written response and a viable payment route is confirmed.

### Written clarification required from Mozilla
Ask:
1. Can an individual whose primary residence and work location during the Fellowship will be Syria receive the 2027–2028 Fellowship grant/stipend?
2. Are there any country-specific legal, sanctions-compliance, or payment restrictions relevant to a Syria-resident applicant?
3. Must the own-name bank account be located in the country of residence, or can an account in another country be used if the applicant is legally entitled to receive the funds while residing/working in Syria?
4. What documentation or screening is required before Mozilla can confirm eligibility?

Do not attempt to bypass restrictions through another person's account, a false residence, or an undisclosed intermediary.

## 2. Track and host

- Track I (Embedded) is the only plausible track to pursue. Track II is framed for senior, recognised innovators with a proven record of independent leadership.
- SRHIN is an official listed Track I host. The applicant must be able to work flexibly across time zones; SRHIN's stated preference is Ibadan/Abuja, Nigeria (WAT, UTC+1).
- The applicant's schedule compatibility must be confirmed honestly.
- The Track I package is $100,000 total: $75,000 stipend and $25,000 project budget. The $125,000 package belongs to Track II.
- An Embedded Fellow is not host staff and is not simply delivering services to existing host programmes. The Fellow collaborates with the host to define an independent project aligned with the host's mission and benefiting the communities it serves.
