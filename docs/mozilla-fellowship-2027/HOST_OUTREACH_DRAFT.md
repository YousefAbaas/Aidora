## Subject
Mozilla Foundation 2027 Embedded Fellowship — exploratory technical collaboration

## Draft

Hello SRHIN team,

I am an Informatics Engineering student and software developer with hands-on experience building a full-stack humanitarian assistance platform called Aidora. I led most of its technical implementation using Flutter/Dart, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, authentication, and role-based workflows. Aidora was an academic project and has not been deployed as a live service, so I would describe it as engineering experience rather than field impact.

I am exploring an application to the Mozilla Foundation 2027–2028 Fellowship, Track I (Embedded Fellows), and I am particularly interested in SRHIN's published challenge around community governance, consent, privacy, and benefit-sharing for mental-health data used in AI.

I have read SRHIN's public descriptions of GENSCORE and GENCurate. I do not want to propose a duplicate data-capture or screening platform, or assume that your current systems lack capabilities. My initial idea is to explore whether an open, interoperable governance layer could help make community-approved data-use permissions, secondary-use review, withdrawal processes, auditability, and benefit-sharing commitments easier to understand and operationalise. This would need to be shaped with SRHIN's technical and clinical teams and, most importantly, the people and communities whose data and experiences are involved.

Would SRHIN be open to an initial conversation about:
1. Whether this is a genuine unmet need alongside your existing GENSCORE/GENCurate work;
2. Whether you would consider an Embedded Fellowship applicant working remotely and flexibly across time zones;
3. What technical experience, community engagement, or language/clinical background you would most value in a potential Fellow?

I understand that the host relationship and project scope would need to be agreed, and that my initial concept may need to change substantially after listening to your team and community partners.

Thank you for considering the enquiry.

Best regards,
[Applicant name]
