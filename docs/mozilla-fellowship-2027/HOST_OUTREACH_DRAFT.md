Dear SRHIN Team,

I am writing to ask whether SRHIN might be open to an exploratory conversation about a potential collaboration under the Mozilla Foundation 2027–2028 Fellowship, specifically its Track I: Embedded Fellows pathway, which lists SRHIN among the potential host organizations.

I am a final-year Informatics Engineering student at Homs University in Syria and the creator and primary technical developer of Aidora, an open-source Flutter/Dart and Django REST Framework project initially designed to help coordinate assistance for people in displacement settings. I have spoken directly with people in need during an early presentation of the project and received encouraging qualitative feedback. To be clear, Aidora has not been deployed as a live humanitarian service, and I do not claim measured field impact or an established user base.

After reviewing SRHIN’s publicly available resources on community governance and co-production, I have been exploring a possible technical direction that would build on—not duplicate—your existing work. The initial hypothesis is that there may be a gap between community governance decisions and the technical systems used to manage data access, reuse, AI development, auditability, and accountability. I would not assume that such a gap exists or that SRHIN needs a new tool: the first step would be to understand your current practices and determine, with your team and relevant communities, whether a specific technical need remains. If existing tools and processes already address it, I would adapt or abandon the proposed direction accordingly.

May I ask:

1. Would SRHIN be open to considering a potential Embedded Fellow collaboration, subject to Mozilla’s selection process and your own assessment?
2. Does this proposed area appear relevant to any current technical or operational need at SRHIN, or would another direction be more useful?
3. Would a fellow be able to work primarily remotely, coordinating with your team in Nigeria?
4. What level of mentorship, team engagement, and participation with affected communities would SRHIN expect from an Embedded Fellow?
5. If there is potential alignment, would you be open to a short introductory online meeting to understand your priorities before I prepare a Letter of Intent?

I want to emphasize that I am not assuming a partnership, endorsement, access to SRHIN systems, or permission to engage communities. I would only develop a proposal further if there is genuine interest and a clearly identified need on your side.

Thank you for considering my inquiry. I would be glad to share a concise project summary and technical evidence from Aidora if useful.
