**Status: draft only. Not sent.**

## Subject

Mozilla Foundation 2027 Embedded Fellowship — exploratory question on community-governed data use

## Draft

Hello SRHIN team,

I am an Informatics Engineering student and software developer. I created and own Aidora, a full-stack humanitarian assistance coordination platform, and led its end-to-end technical design and principal implementation using Flutter/Dart, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, role-aware workflows, automated tests, and GitHub Actions delivery pipelines. Aidora was developed in an academic context and has not been deployed as a live humanitarian service; I present it as evidence of engineering capability, not as field impact.

I am exploring an application to the Mozilla Foundation 2027–2028 Fellowship, Track I (Embedded Fellows), and am interested in the challenge described in the Fellowship RFP around community-owned governance, consent, privacy, and benefit-sharing for mental-health data used in AI.

I have also reviewed SRHIN's public work, including GENSCORE, GENCurate, the LEEF pre-launch checklist, and your guide for people with lived experience to participate in AI mental-health tool design. I do not want to duplicate these initiatives or assume that your current systems lack appropriate safeguards.

My initial hypothesis is narrower: would there be value in exploring an open, machine-readable way to carry community-defined data-use conditions into downstream access, secondary research, AI training/evaluation, audit, withdrawal, and benefit-sharing workflows? A small policy manifest and reference policy-checking service might be useful, but only if it addresses a real gap identified by your team and the communities involved.

Would you be open to clarifying:
1. Whether this is a genuine unmet need alongside your existing technical, ethical, and community-governance work;
2. Whether you would consider an Embedded Fellow working remotely and flexibly across time zones;
3. Which technical, community-engagement, language, or health-governance capabilities you would most value in a potential Fellow;
4. Whether there is an existing workflow or public resource you would recommend reviewing before proposing a project scope?

I understand that any Fellowship project would need to be shaped with SRHIN and the people whose data and experiences are involved. I would welcome a brief exploratory conversation if this direction is relevant.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,  
Yousef Abbas
